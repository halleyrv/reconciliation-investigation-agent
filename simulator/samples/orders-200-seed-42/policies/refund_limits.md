# Refund Limits Policy

A refund is issued after its payment is captured and within the refund window counted in calendar days from the capture.

Refund window: 5 calendar days

A payment is refunded at most once, fully or partially, in USD. The refunded amount is at least 0.01 and never exceeds the captured amount of the payment. A second refund of the same payment is not allowed.

A refund is netted in a later settlement batch of the gateway that captured the payment, never in the batch of the payment itself.
