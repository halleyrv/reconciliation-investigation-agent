# Dispute Handling Policy

A dispute is a customer's challenge of a captured payment raised through the gateway. The merchant answers each dispute with its evidence (order, payment, refund and authorization records) within the response window, counted in business days from the day the gateway reports the dispute.

Response window: 7 business days

Bank holidays: 2026-10-09, 2026-10-20, 2026-10-28

While a dispute is open, the disputed amount is not refunded a second time and no adjustment is booked for it. An unanswered dispute is lost by default.
