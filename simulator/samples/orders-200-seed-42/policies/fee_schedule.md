# Gateway Fee Schedule

All fees are charged in USD on each captured payment: a percentage in basis points plus a fixed amount, rounded once to cents with ROUND_HALF_EVEN. Fees are not returned when a payment is refunded.

| Gateway | Percentage (bps) | Fixed fee |

| gateway_a | 290 | 0.30 |
| gateway_b | 250 | 0.25 |
